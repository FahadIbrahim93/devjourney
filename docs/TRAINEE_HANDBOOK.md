# Trainee Handbook - Business & Marketing

Welcome to **Hope Theory**! This handbook explains what we do, how the system works, what is expected of you, and your first 4 weeks. Read it once fully, then keep it open as a reference.

> **The 3 golden rules**
> 1. **Never contact a client, share a price, or share a file without Hope's okay.**
> 2. **Never spend money** (no ads, no paid tools, no domain purchases).
> 3. **Report to Hope, not to the bots.** Hope verifies your work and decides what happens next.

## 1. What the agency does
Hope Theory builds **websites for small businesses** (studios, clinics, restaurants, schools, shops), mostly in Dhaka. We handle the design, the build, launch on the client's domain, and a handoff so the client owns their site.

- **First client:** **Client A**, a small design studio in Dhaka. The real name and contacts are in Notion (Clients); this repo is public, so we always write **Client A** here and in GitHub.
- **Target launch:** 27 October 2026.
- **How we make money:** a fixed project price paid in milestones (deposit → design approval → before launch), plus optional maintenance later.

Your job: help the **business side** grow and run well. That means research, content, marketing drafts, lead lists and client-facing materials (which Hope checks first).

## 3. Roles and chain of command
| Who | Role |
|---|---|
| **Hope** (Fahad Ibrahim) | Founder. Teaches you, assigns your work, **verifies everything you deliver**, talks to clients, sets prices. |
| **You (Trainee)** | Business & marketing tasks. You report to Hope. |

You **do not** talk to the bots directly, and you don't need access to them.

## 4. How to take a task
1. Open Notion **Tasks**, filter **Assignee = Trainee**. Start with the earliest **Due** date (or what Hope says is most urgent).
2. Open the task page (and its **GitHub issue** link if it has one) and read everything: *Why this matters*, *Goal*, *Steps*, *Deliverable*, *What good looks like*.
3. Unclear? **Ask Hope before starting.** A 2-minute question saves 2 hours.
4. Set the Notion Status to **Doing**.
5. Follow the steps. Save your work in Google Drive, in a folder Hope gives you, with clear names (e.g. `Competitor scan - Oct 2026`).
6. Check your work against **What good looks like**.
7. Report to Hope (next section). Hope ticks **Verified by Hope** and sets **Done**.

## 5. How to report
**For each task:** send Hope a short message:
> **Task:** Competitor scan · **Status:** done
> **Link:** (Google Sheet link)
> **Key findings:** 1) … 2) … 3) …
> **Time spent:** 4h · **Questions/blockers:** …

**Weekly report (every Saturday evening), copy this template:**
```
WEEKLY REPORT — week of [date]
1. Done this week: (task + link)
2. Results & numbers: (e.g. 30 leads found, 12 with no website; 10 competitors reviewed)
3. Blockers: (what stopped you, what you need from Hope)
4. Next week: (top 3 tasks you plan to do)
5. What I learned: (1–2 lines)
```

## 6. Quality standards
- **Accurate:** check every name, link and number. **Never invent** facts, reviews or contact details. If you don't know, write "unknown".
- **Complete:** fill every column/section, or explain why it's blank.
- **Clear:** headings, short sentences, a summary at the top, top 3 recommendations.
- **Professional:** correct spelling (English and Bangla), consistent formatting, readable on a phone.
- **On time:** if you'll miss a due date, tell Hope **before** the date, not after.
- **Sources:** note where information came from (link).

## 7. Rules
1. **Clients:** never message, call or email a client or lead, and never post publicly about them, without Hope's written okay. Drafts are fine; sending is Hope's job.
2. **Prices and files:** never share prices, proposals, invoices or client files with anyone outside the agency.
3. **Money:** no spending of any kind. Free tools only (Google Docs/Sheets, Canva free, Notion, Facebook, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile).
4. **Confidentiality:** client details, payments and internal plans stay inside the agency, including with friends and at university. Don't screenshot client data to others.
5. **Accounts:** don't change settings in Notion, GitHub, Google or social accounts. Don't delete anything.
6. **Honesty:** never make up numbers, testimonials or claims in marketing drafts.
7. **Family-client etiquette:** our first client is family-connected. Be respectful and formal in anything written for her. Business stays business: prices, deadlines and feedback go through Hope, not through family conversations. Don't discuss the project with relatives. If a relative asks about it, say "please ask Hope".
