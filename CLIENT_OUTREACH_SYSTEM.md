## 🎯 THE OUTREACH PHILOSOPHY

**Golden Rule:** Every message must lead with value, not ask for something.

**Bad:** "Hi, I'm a developer. Hire me."
**Good:** "I saw your post about X. I built something similar for Y. Here's what I learned that might help you."

**Structure:**
1. Reference something specific about them (shows you're not spamming)
2. Add value with a specific insight or resource
3. Soft CTA (ask a question, not for a call)
