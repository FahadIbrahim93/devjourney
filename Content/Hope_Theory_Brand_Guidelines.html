Color Usage

Use Void Black (#0a0a0f) as the primary background. Cyan is the primary accent — use for links, highlights, borders. Purple for secondary accents and gradients. Green for positive states (success, growth, crypto up). Pink sparingly for CTAs and emphasis. Text should be near-white (#e8e8f0) on dark backgrounds.

Brand Applications

X / Social Media

Dark profile with cyan accent. Banner shows the brand grid or code/matrix aesthetic. Use ankh (☤) as watermark. No human photos. Quote retweets > original posts for engagement.

Website

Dark mode-first. Animated grid background. Content-focused layout with high contrast typography. Ventures shown as a grid of cards with neon hover effects.

Newsletter / Content

Markdown-first. Dark-themed code blocks. Bold headers with gradient treatment. Minimal images — let the text carry the weight. Direct email style, no templates.

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

Use dark backgrounds with neon accents. Let content breathe with generous whitespace. Apply the gradient to headlines and important elements. Keep imagery abstract, futuristic, grid-like. Use the ankh as a subtle brand mark.

✗ Don't

Use human faces or figures in brand imagery. Apply gradients to body text. Use bright/light backgrounds for primary content. Mix in corporate stock photography. Use more than 3 accent colors in a single composition.
