# 📅 WEEKLY REVIEW SYSTEM
## Every Sunday. 15 Minutes. Keeps You On Track.

**Why:** We drifted for 15 hours without course-correcting.
**Fix:** This 15-minute Sunday ritual.

---

## THE 7 QUESTIONS

### Q1: Am I closer to the goal than last week?

| Last week | This week | Change |
|-----------|-----------|--------|
| X followers | ___ | ___ |
| LinkedIn connections | ___ | ___ |
| Applications sent | ___ | ___ |
| Responses received | ___ | ___ |
| Interviews | ___ | ___ |
| Revenue | ___ | ___ |

If 3+ metrics are flat or down → Something needs to change.

---

### Q2: What was the HIGHEST-impact action this week?

One thing that moved the needle most.
_Write it down. Do more of this._

---

### Q3: What was the LOWEST-impact action this week?

One thing that wasted time.
_Write it down. Do less of this._

---

### Q4: Is my PRIMARY goal still the same?

```
Last week's goal: ________________________________
This week's goal:  ________________________________
Changed? ☐ Yes  ☐ No
```

If it changed → Re-run the GOAL_CLARIFICATION_FRAMEWORK.

---

### Q5: What's the ONE thing I must accomplish next week?

Not 10 things. ONE thing.
If I do nothing else, this one thing makes the week a win.

---

### Q6: What's blocking me?

```
Biggest blocker right now: ________________________________
What I need to remove it:  ________________________________
```

---

### Q7: Am I measuring output or outcome?

```
Output this week (files, posts, hours):    ________
Outcome this week (interviews, $, leads): ________
```

If output > outcome → Stop producing. Start converting.

---

## THE WEEKLY RITUAL (15 min)

```
SUNDAY EVENING

7:00 PM — Open this document
7:02 PM — Answer Q1 (metrics check)
7:04 PM — Answer Q2 + Q3 (best/worst actions)
7:06 PM — Answer Q4 (goal still right?)
7:07 PM — Answer Q5 (ONE priority for next week)
7:08 PM — Answer Q6 (blockers)
7:09 PM — Answer Q7 (output vs outcome)
7:10 PM — Plan Monday's 3 most important tasks
7:15 PM — Done. Rest.

Total: 15 minutes. Non-negotiable.
```

---

## MONTHLY DEEP DIVE (30 min)

First Sunday of every month, add these:

### Trend Analysis
```
Metric trend over 30 days:
📈 Improving → Keep doing what works
📉 Declining → Change strategy
➡️ Flat → Need more volume or different approach
```

### Strategy Review
```
Is the current platform mix right?
Should I double down on one platform?
Is my content resonating with the target audience?
```

### Asset Inventory
```
What assets do I have? (files, projects, content)
What's outdated or irrelevant? → Archive or delete
What's my best-performing piece? → Repurpose it
```

---

## THE RULE

**If you don't measure it, you can't improve it.**
**If you don't review it weekly, you'll drift.**

15 minutes on Sunday saves 15 hours of wrong-direction work.

---

*Weekly review = the difference between intentional progress and random activity.* 📊
