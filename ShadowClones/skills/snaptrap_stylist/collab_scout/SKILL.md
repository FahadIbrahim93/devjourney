# Collab Scout

## Purpose
Find and vet collaboration partners — brands, creators, music artists — that align with SnapTrap's brand DNA and audience.

## Trigger Conditions
- Quarterly collab planning
- User asks "who should we work with?"
- New product needs buzz
- On-demand via `/collab_scout <goal>`

## Required Inputs
- **Collab goal** (awareness, credibility, new audience, sales)
- **Budget** (gifted, co-funded, 50/50, full)
- **Collab type** (co-designed piece, content only, event, cross-promo)

## Steps

### 1. Define Collab Strategy
- **Brand collabs:** Co-designed piece, both brands promote
- **Creator collabs:** Limited edition with their name/logo, they promote to their audience
- **Music collabs:** Wear it in a video, get featured, no co-design
- **Event collabs:** Pop-up, market, festival

### 2. Brainstorm Collab Categories

**Adjacent brands (jewelry, accessories):**
- Marjahans (our own brand) — but doesn't make sense
- Other streetwear brands (Samsøe, Stüssy, Obey)
- Local artists (BD: Baul, Fakira-inspired)
- Skate brands, surf brands (subculture overlap)

**Creators (visual artists, designers, photographers):**
- Local mural artists
- Graphic designers with cultural roots
- Photographers with streetwear aesthetic
- TikTok creators in streetwear niche (10K-100K)

**Music artists (Bengali, hip-hop, indie):**
- Underground hip-hop (credibility)
- Baul fusion (cultural)
- BD rappers (Anupam, traditional)
- Producers (art for merch)

**Lifestyle brands (cigars, coffee, beer):**
- Coffee roasters (premium lifestyle)
- Vinyl shops (culture alignment)
- Indie magazines (cross-promo)

### 3. Vet Each Partner

**Audience overlap (1-10):**
- Demographics match (age, location, income)
- Aesthetic alignment (do they look like our customer?)
- Values match (sustainability, culture, premium)

**Reach (1-10):**
- Their audience size
- Engagement quality (not just followers)
- Conversion potential (will their audience buy?)

**Authenticity (1-10):**
- Do they actually use products like yours?
- Will promotion feel organic or forced?
- Past collabs: quality, reception

**Cost (1-10 ROI):**
- Total investment
- Expected return
- Time to recoup

### 4. Outreach Template

```
Hey [name],

I'm [your name] from SnapTrap. I've been following your work on [platform] — your [specific project/series] really stood out. The way you [specific detail] is exactly the kind of cultural storytelling we want to be part of.

We're a streetwear brand inspired by [heritage], and I think there's a real opportunity to do something together. I'm thinking a co-designed [item] that brings together [your aesthetic] and [our aesthetic].

Would love to explore this. Happy to share more about us if you're interested. No pressure either way — your work speaks for itself.

[Your name]
SnapTrap
[link]
```

### 5. Co-Design Process (if green-lit)

**Timeline:**
- Week 1: Kickoff call, define scope
- Week 2: Design concepts (both submit, then merge)
- Week 3: Revisions, finalize
- Week 4-8: Production
- Week 9: Launch
- Week 10: Co-promotion (both accounts)
- Week 11+: Repost UGC, follow-up

**Revenue split:**
- Co-design: 50/50 on net profit (after production)
- Creator piece: 70/30 (creator gets 30% royalty on top of production cost)
- Straight up: 50/50, both pay 50% of production

### 6. Post-Collab Measurement
- Units sold
- Revenue (total, attributable)
- New followers / subscribers gained
- Press / media coverage
- Audience sentiment (DMs, comments, sentiment analysis)

## Common Pitfalls
- ❌ Collabing for the numbers (mismatched audience)
- ❌ No clear split (resentment later)
- ❌ Vague agreement (he said/she said)
- ❌ Choosing biggest names (often worst fit)
- ❌ No contract / written agreement
- ❌ Post-collab silence (don't nurture the relationship)
