# Community Builder

## Purpose
Build a real community around SnapTrap. Convert followers into superfans who buy every drop, defend the brand, and recruit others.

## Trigger Conditions
- User asks "how do I build community?"
- Pre-drop (build hype)
- Post-drop (keep momentum)
- Quarterly community audit
- On-demand via `/community_builder`

## Required Inputs
- **Current community size** (IG followers, Discord members, etc.)
- **Engagement level** (low, medium, high)
- **Goal** (size, engagement, sales, brand defense)

## Steps

### 1. Community Pyramid
- **Top — Superfans (1-3%):** Buy every drop, post UGC, defend brand
- **Middle — Engaged (10-15%):** Comment, share, attend IRL events
- **Bottom — Lurkers (80-85%):** Follow, watch, don't engage
- **Goal:** Move people UP the pyramid

### 2. Choose Your Platforms

**Discord (best for superfans):**
- Real-time chat
- Voice channels
- Roles (VIP, OG, etc.)
- Polls, giveaways
- Best for: Inner circle, pre-drop access

**Telegram (best for updates):**
- One-way broadcasts (channel) + two-way (group)
- Low friction (no phone number shared)
- Best for: Drop alerts, quick updates

**Instagram (best for reach):**
- Discovery engine
- Stories for daily
- Reels for growth
- DMs for personal touch
- Best for: Awareness, new follower acquisition

**X / Twitter (best for culture):**
- Streetwear conversation
- Brand voice, opinions
- Engage with other brands, creators
- Best for: Cultural relevance

**TikTok (best for virality):**
- Reels-style content
- Sound trends
- Best for: Top-of-funnel reach

### 3. Engagement Tactics

**Daily:**
- Reply to every comment (first hour)
- DM 5 new followers per day (personal touch)
- Story: Behind-the-scenes, polls, "this or that"

**Weekly:**
- 1 community-only post (Discord/Telegram)
- 1 AMA or "Ask Me Anything"
- Shoutout to best community content
- Run a poll: "What should we drop next?"

**Monthly:**
- IRL meet-up (if city warrants)
- Community vote on next drop theme
- Exclusive content / early access

**Pre-Drop:**
- 4 weeks: Concept teaser, community reaction
- 2 weeks: "Your turn" — let them vote on color/graphic
- 1 week: Countdown + early-access link (Discord only)

**Post-Drop:**
- "Thank you" post (community first)
- Repost every UGC (with credit)
- Highlight top buyers
- "What's next?" tease

### 4. Convert Lurkers → Engaged
- Ask questions in captions (not just "Link in bio")
- Reply to DMs personally (no bots)
- "Tag a friend who…" (low-friction engagement)
- Run a low-stakes giveaway (e.g., "Repost to win")
- Show up consistently (3+ posts/week)

### 5. Convert Engaged → Superfans
- Spot engaged followers, invite to Discord
- DM them personally, ask opinion
- Feature their UGC on main account
- Give them early access / discounts
- Send them free product (random acts)
- Invite to virtual / IRL events

### 6. UGC Strategy
- **Hashtag:** #SnapTrapStyle, #SnapTrapDrip
- **Repost:** Every UGC (with credit)
- **Incentivize:** Monthly UGC contest, winner gets free piece
- **Feature:** "Customer of the month" story
- **UGC gallery:** Section on website
- **Reward:** First 50 customers get a "FOUNDING" patch

### 7. Crisis Management
- Quality complaint → respond fast, make it right publicly
- Bad review → don't delete, respond professionally
- Brand controversy → take a position (silence is worse)
- Customer disappointment → over-communicate

**Week 1: Foundation**
- Set up Telegram channel (drop alerts)
- Create Discord onboarding flow (new member → 7-day welcome)

**Week 2: Engagement**
- Daily stories with questions (poll, this-or-that, "rate this fit 1-10")
- Reply to every IG comment in first hour
- 1 Discord AMA (live, 30 min)

**Week 3: Pre-Drop Hype**
- Announce next drop theme in Discord (24h before IG)

- Highlight 5 UGC posts on main account
- "Founding member" patch for first 50 customers (next drop)

---

**MEASUREMENT (30 days):**
- Discord members: 320 → 500 (+180)

- IG engagement: 3.2% → 4.0%+
- UGC posts: ~20 → 60+

## Common Pitfalls
- ❌ Posting but never replying
- ❌ Buying followers (kills engagement)
- ❌ Treating community as sales channel
- ❌ No crisis plan (small issue → viral disaster)
- ❌ Inconsistent presence (3 posts one week, none the next)
- ❌ Ignoring lurkers (they're the future superfans)
