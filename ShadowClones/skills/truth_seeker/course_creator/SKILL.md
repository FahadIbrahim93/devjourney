# Course Creator

## Purpose
Design and launch a profitable online course. From outline to curriculum to delivery to launch. Teach what you know.

## Trigger Conditions
- User wants to monetize expertise
- Audience is asking "how do I learn [X]?"
- Time to scale beyond 1:1 work
- On-demand via `/course_creator <topic>`

## Required Inputs
- **Topic** (what will you teach)
- **Audience** (who needs this)
- **Expertise level** (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
- **Format** (video, cohort, hybrid, async)
- **Price target** (free, $50, $500, $2000)

## Steps

### 1. Validate Demand
Before building, validate:
- **Search volume:** "how to [topic]" Google Trends, YouTube search
- **Existing audience:** Are people asking you about this?
- **Competitor courses:** Are there already successful courses?
  - If yes: different angle (better, cheaper, deeper)
  - If no: maybe no market
- **Pre-sell:** Can you get 10 deposits before building?
- **Survey:** Ask your audience, "Would you pay $X for a course that does Y?"

**Rule:** Build what people are already asking for, not what you want to teach.

### 2. Course Outline

**For each module:**
- **Title:** Outcome-focused ("Build your first DeFi strategy")
- **Lessons:** 3-7 per module
- **Length:** 5-15 min per lesson
- **Assignment:** 1 per module (optional)
- **Resource:** 1 per module (template, checklist, etc.)

**Total length:**
- Mini-course (paid): 2-4 hours
- Standard course: 6-12 hours
- Flagship course: 20-40 hours

### 3. Module + Lesson Design

**Module structure (1.5-3 hours each):**
1. **Hook:** "By the end of this module, you'll be able to..."
2. **Context:** Why this matters
3. **Core concepts:** The frameworks
4. **Walkthrough:** Step-by-step
5. **Real example:** Case study
6. **Assignment:** Apply it
7. **Recap:** Quick summary

**Lesson structure (5-15 min each):**
1. **Hook (30s):** What you'll learn + why
2. **Setup (1-2 min):** Context
3. **Content (5-10 min):** Main material
4. **Recap (30s):** Key takeaway
5. **CTA (30s):** What's next

### 4. Production

**Pre-production:**
- Script (don't wing it)
- Slides / visuals (clean, branded)
- Talking points (1-pager per lesson)
- Demo prep (test all software)
- Backup slides (Q&A)

**Recording:**
- Mic: Decent ($50-200 USB mic)
- Camera: 1080p (webcam is fine)
- Lighting: Ring light or window
- Background: Clean, branded
- Screen recording: OBS (free) or Loom
- Teleprompter: PromptSmart or your script on second monitor

**Post-production:**
- Edit: CapCut (free), Descript (great for beginners), Camtasia
- Add captions: 80% of videos watched muted (auto-captions essential)
- Add visuals: B-roll, diagrams, animations
- Music: Subtle background (Epidemic Sound, $15/mo)
- Thumbnail: 1280x720, high contrast, text overlay

**Quality bar:**
- Audio quality > video quality
- 5 minutes of editing per 1 minute of video
- Watch the WHOLE thing before publishing

### 5. Platform + Delivery

**Self-host (full control, more work):**
- Teachable, Thinkific, Kajabi ($30-200/mo)
- Your own site + Stripe
- You control pricing, branding, data

**Marketplace (less control, more reach):**
- Udemy (huge reach, low price ceiling, takes 50%+ on sales)
- Skillshare (subscription model, low payout)
- Coursera (high barrier, university-style)

**Cohort-based (premium, more support):**
- Maven ($1K-10K courses, live cohort)
- Circle + Zoom (your own platform, $500-2K)
- Disco ($1K+ courses)

**Recommendation for first course:** Self-host on Teachable ($39/mo) or use your own site. You keep 90%+ of revenue.

### 6. Pricing Strategy

**Free:**
- Lead magnet
- Builds trust
- Email list growth
- Limited revenue

**$50-200:**
- Mini-course, low commitment
- High conversion rate
- Modest revenue
- Good for: "Test before you invest"

**$200-1,000:**
- Standard course
- Sweet spot for most
- Mid-tier commitment
- Good for: 90% of courses

**$1,000-5,000:**
- Premium / cohort
- Higher touch
- Lower volume needed
- Good for: B2B, career-changers

**$5,000+:**
- Flagship, mentorship-style
- 1:1 elements
- Brand-building more than revenue
- Good for: Established experts

**Rule:** Price for the value delivered, not the time spent. A 2-hour course that saves 100 hours of confusion = $500 easily.

### 7. Launch Sequence

**Pre-launch (4-6 weeks before):**
- Waitlist page (collect emails)
- Behind-the-scenes content
- 2-3 free preview lessons
- Build anticipation

**Launch week (5-7 days):**
- Day 1: Email #1 (announce, what it is, why now)
- Day 2: Email #2 (your story, what you'll learn)
- Day 3: Social media push
- Day 4: Email #3 (curriculum walkthrough)
- Day 5: Email #4 (testimonials / case studies)
- Day 6: Email #5 (FAQ, objections)
- Day 7: Email #6 (last chance, doors close)

**Post-launch:**
- Cart open for 1 week, then close
- Cohort starts 1-2 weeks later
- 1-2 weekly emails during course
- Office hours (live Q&A)

### 8. Evergreen vs Cohort

**Evergreen (always available):**
- More revenue, less urgency
- More customer support (always)
- Easier to scale
- Can do via email automation

**Cohort (start dates):**
- Higher completion rates
- Premium pricing justified
- Community element
- More work (live elements)

**Recommendation:** Start cohort, convert to evergreen once polished.

## Common Pitfalls
- ❌ Building before validating
- ❌ Course on a topic you don't have authority in
- ❌ 40+ hours of content (bloat, low completion)
- ❌ No assignment / community (low completion)
- ❌ Pricing too low ("course" mindset = undervalue)
- ❌ No launch sequence (just put it up)
- ❌ Low audio quality (people leave)
- ❌ No follow-up (one launch, no evergreen)
