# Trend Synthesizer

## Purpose
Connect today's events to historical patterns and macro trends. Spot the undercurrents. Make sense of chaos.

## Trigger Conditions
- Daily research cron
- User asks "what's really happening?"
- Big news event
- Weekly synthesis
- On-demand via `/trend_synthesizer <topic>`

## Required Inputs
- **Topic** (specific event or theme)
- **Time horizon** (current, 1-2 years, 5-10 years, multi-decade)
- **Lens** (economic, cultural, technological, political)

## Steps

### 1. Multi-Lens Analysis

Look at every trend through 4 lenses:

**Economic lens:**
- Money supply, inflation, interest rates
- Asset prices, real estate, crypto
- Employment, productivity, growth
- Debt levels, leverage

**Cultural lens:**
- Generational shifts (Gen Z, Gen Alpha)
- Values (sustainability, individualism, community)
- Aesthetics (Y2K, brutalism, cottagecore)
- Religion, philosophy, identity

**Technological lens:**
- AI, automation, computing
- Energy (solar, fusion, batteries)
- Biotech, life extension
- Space, materials science

**Political lens:**
- Power blocs (US, China, multipolar)
- Trade, sanctions, alliances
- Domestic politics (populism, polarization)
- Geopolitical risk (war, conflict)

### 2. Historical Pattern Matching

For current events, find parallels:

**Monetary:**
- 1970s stagflation
- 2008 GFC
- 1929 Great Depression
- 2000 dot-com
- 2020 COVID

**Tech cycles:**
- 1995-2000 internet
- 2007-2012 mobile
- 2010-2020 cloud
- 2017+ crypto
- 2023+ AI

**Cultural:**
- 1960s counterculture
- 1980s materialism
- 1990s irony
- 2000s excess
- 2010s authenticity
- 2020s+ ?

### 3. Identify Cycles
**Cycles to track:**

**K-wave (Kondratiev):** 40-60 year economic cycles
**Business cycle:** 5-10 year expansions/contractions
**Credit cycle:** 7-10 year boom/bust
**Real estate:** 18 year boom/bust
**Decade theme:** 2010s = mobile/social, 2020s = AI/debt, 2030s = ?

### 4. Synthesis Framework

For each trend/pattern, output:
- **What's happening** (current state)
- **Why now** (triggers, catalysts)
- **Historical parallel** (most similar past event)
- **What's different this time** (4 DWT)
- **Where this goes** (12-24 month outlook)
- **Implication for [topic]** (actionable insight)

### 5. The 4 DWT Framework

For "this time is different" claims, check:
- **Denominations:** Same unit, different number
- **Deleveraging:** Debt cycle
- **Demographics:** Population dynamics
- **Distribution:** Tech access (e.g., mobile internet 2020s vs desktop 2000s)

### 6. Track the Narratives

Every era has dominant narratives:
- 1990s: "End of history" (Fukuyama)
- 2000s: "Housing never goes down"
- 2010s: "Tech will save us"
- 2020s: "AI will replace us" / "Bitcoin is digital gold"

**When a narrative dominates, it's near its peak.** Counter-narratives emerge.

### 7. Cross-Domain Synthesis

The biggest insights come from connecting across domains:
- **AI + inequality:** Productivity gains concentrated
- **Crypto + monetary policy:** Decentralized alternative
- **Climate + insurance:** Risk uninsurable
- **Demographics + housing:** Aging rich, fewer buyers

## Common Pitfalls
- ❌ Single-lens analysis (just economic, just tech)
- ❌ "This time is different" (almost always wrong)
- ❌ Forever-bullish or forever-bearish
- ❌ Missing cross-domain connections
- ❌ No actionable implications
- ❌ Cherry-picking data
- ❌ No update cadence (synthesis is point-in-time)
