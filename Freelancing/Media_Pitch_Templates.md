**Hook:** [One sentence that makes them go "Wait, what?" - specific, surprising, relevant to their audience]

**The Story:** [2-3 sentences explaining WHY this matters NOW]
- Data point/trend from your research
- Gap in current coverage
- Why their audience needs this

**Your Unique Angle:** [What YOU bring that others don't]
- Specific experience/results (numbers!)
- Proprietary framework/methodology
- Access to exclusive data/community

**Concrete Deliverable:** [What they'll actually publish]

**About Me:** [2-sentence credibility builder]

**Next Steps:** [Clear, low-friction CTA]
