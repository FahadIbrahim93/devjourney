## CONSULTING BEST PRACTICES & TIPS

### Positioning Your Consulting Practice:
- Focus on specific industries or problem domains where you have deep expertise
- Develop proprietary frameworks or methodologies that differentiate you
- Emphasize outcomes and ROI rather than activities or deliverables
- Build thought leadership through content, speaking, and publications
- Package services into clearly defined offerings with predictable pricing

### Delivering Exceptional Value:
- Always start with clear objectives and success metrics
- Use structured approaches but remain flexible to client needs
- Combine data-driven analysis with practical, actionable recommendations
- Deliver insights, not just information
- Prepare thoroughly for workshops and interviews
- Follow the "tell them what you'll tell them, tell them, tell them what you told them" structure
- Provide executive summaries for busy stakeholders
- Include implementation considerations in all recommendations

### Managing Client Expectations:
- Clearly define what consulting is and isn't (advice vs. implementation)
- Set realistic timelines for complex analyses
- Be honest about limitations and areas where you may need to bring in specialists
- Under-promise and over-deliver on insights and actionability
- Provide regular check-ins on longer engagements
- Be ready to say "I don't know, but I'll find out" or "Let me bring in expertise on that"

### Pricing Strategies:
- Value-based pricing: Tie fees to client outcomes or savings when measurable
- Retainer models: Provide predictable income and deeper client relationships
- Tiered offerings: Create entry-level, standard, and premium packages
- Packaging: Bundle related services for increased value perception
- Discounts: Consider for long-term commitments, referrals, or non-profit work

### Red Flags in Consulting Engagements:
- Client unwilling to define clear objectives or success criteria
- Constantly shifting goals or scope without discussion
- Resistance to providing access to necessary information or stakeholders
- Expectation of guaranteed results from advisory work
- Requests for significant unpaid work ("just give me your thoughts")
- History of not paying consultants or disputing fees
- Pressure to violate ethical standards or provide misleading advice
- Unwillingness to implement reasonable recommendations

### Exit Strategies:
- Define success criteria for engagement completion
- Plan knowledge transfer to internal teams
- Suggest next steps or ongoing support options
- Request feedback and testimonials upon completion
- Leave door open for future engagements
- Consider phased approach for large transformations
