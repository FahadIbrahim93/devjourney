## RETAINER TIPS & BEST PRACTICES

### For Freelancers:
- Set clear boundaries on what's included vs. extra
- Track time meticulously to justify value
- Proactively suggest improvements and optimizations
- Review and adjust scope quarterly based on actual usage
- Consider tiered retainer levels (e.g., Basic, Standard, Premium)

### For Clients:
- Clearly communicate priorities each month
- Provide timely feedback to maximize efficiency
- Consider quarterly planning sessions to align on goals
- Review usage reports to optimize retainer level
- Provide access to necessary systems and stakeholders

### Common Retainer Structures:
1. **Hours-Based**: X hours/month at set rate
2. **Availability-Based**: Guaranteed response/availability
3. **Outcome-Based**: Specific monthly deliverables/objectives
4. **Hybrid**: Base hours + availability guarantee

### Red Flags to Watch:
- Client consistently requesting work outside scope without approving extra charges
- Unclear or constantly changing priorities
- Resistance to tracking or reporting usage
- Requests for significant discounts on long-term commitment without scope definition
