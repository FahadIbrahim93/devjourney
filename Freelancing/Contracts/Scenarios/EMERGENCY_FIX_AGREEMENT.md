## 1. SCOPE OF EMERGENCY SERVICES
Freelancer agrees to provide emergency technical support services to address critical, production-impacting issues that require immediate attention outside of normal business hours or standard support channels.

**Emergency Issues Qualify As:**
- Complete system outage affecting core functionality
- Security breaches or vulnerabilities being actively exploited
- Data loss or corruption incidents
- Critical payment processing failures
- Issues preventing access to essential business functions
- Other issues mutually agreed upon as "emergency" in writing

**Does NOT Qualify As Emergency:**
- Feature requests or enhancements
- Performance improvements (unless causing complete failure)
- Minor bugs not affecting core functionality
- Planned maintenance or updates
- Issues occurring during agreed maintenance windows
