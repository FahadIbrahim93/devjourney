## KEY OBSERVATIONS (FY2024-25)

### Positive Trends:
1. **Profitability Improved Significantly:** Net profit more than doubled from BDT 104.70M to 232.74M (122% increase)
2. **Margins Expanded:** Gross margin improved from 7.00% to 9.12%; Net margin from 1.85% to 4.14%
3. **Liquidity Strengthened:** Current ratio improved from 1.62 to 1.70
4. **Strong Recovery:** Best profit after tax in the last 6 years
5. **Dividend Declared:** 10% cash dividend recommended

### Concerns:
1. **Revenue Slight Decline:** Revenue decreased marginally from BDT 5,674.59M to 5,621.62M (-0.93%)
2. **Assets Slightly Reduced:** Total assets decreased by 0.29%

## MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION HIGHLIGHTS

- Bangladesh now produces >900 million meters denim fabric annually, leading global market
- Industry faces challenges: inflation, energy costs, supply chain disruptions
- Company focusing on sustainability (LEED-certified operations) and cost optimization
- Solar power plant installed for green energy
- Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) for environmental compliance
- Focus on equity-based financing to reduce interest rate risk
