## HOW WE WORK

### The 4-Phase Delivery Process

**Phase 1 — Discovery (1-2 days)**
Understand your goals, not just the technical requirements. What does success look like for your users? What constraints exist? What does "done" actually mean?

**Phase 2 — Architecture & Plan (2-3 days)**
Clear scope, technical approach, and milestones. You'll receive a written plan with acceptance criteria before a single line of production code is written.

**Phase 3 — Build (per agreed timeline)**
Working increments delivered at regular intervals. You see the code as it progresses — not as a final surprise. Every commit passes quality gates.

**Phase 4 — Deliver & Stabilize (1-2 days)**
Production deployment, final QA, documentation handover, and knowledge transfer. We don't disappear after delivery.
