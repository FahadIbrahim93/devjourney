## 🎯 HOW TO USE

1. Choose the template matching your client's industry
2. Replace all `[PLACEHOLDERS]` with client-specific details

## 🎨 PROPOSAL BEST PRACTICES

1. **Keep it under 2 pages** — clients skim, they don't read
2. **Lead with value** — what they get, not what you do
3. **Be specific** — "$5,000" not "affordable"
4. **Add guarantee** — reduces risk perception
5. **Soft CTA** — "Questions? Let's jump on a call" not "Sign here"
6. **Use their language** — mirror their job post/email
