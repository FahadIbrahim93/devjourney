## Finding Clients (Vibe Coder Angle)

### Cold Outreach Template
```
Hey [Name],

I noticed [company] is building [something]. 
I'm a solo developer specializing in [your stack] + AI-assisted development.

I can build [specific thing] in [timeframe] at [rate].

Quick question: What's your biggest technical challenge right now?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### Fiverr Gig Template
- **Title:** "I will build your [specific app] with AI assistance"
- **Description:** Fast delivery, clean code, modern stack
- **Packages:** Basic ($50), Standard ($150), Premium ($300)
- **Extras:** Extra fast delivery, Source code, Documentation

---

## Client Communication Rules

1. **Response within 2 hours** during work hours
2. **Daily updates** on progress
3. **Never say no** - say "let me figure that out"
4. **Document everything** in writing
5. **Get paid** - milestone payments for big projects
