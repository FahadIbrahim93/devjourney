## 🎯 PROPOSAL TIPS

1. **Personalize** — use their name, company, project details
2. **Be specific** — "$5,000" not "affordable"
3. **Add timeline** — shows you're organized
4. **Include guarantee** — reduces risk
5. **Soft CTA** — "Questions? Let's chat" not "Sign here"

---

## 📝 CONTRACT TIPS

1. Always use a contract — no exceptions
2. Include Exhibit A with detailed scope
3. Set clear payment terms
4. Define revision limits
5. Include IP transfer clause

---

## 💰 INVOICE TIPS

1. Invoice immediately after contract signed
2. Include PO number if provided
3. State payment terms clearly
4. Send payment reminders
5. Thank them for payment
