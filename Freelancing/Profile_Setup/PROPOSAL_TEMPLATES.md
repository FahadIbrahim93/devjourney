# PROPOSAL TEMPLATES

> 5-part winning formula based on 2026 Upwork research:
> 1. Open with their problem
> 2. Show relevant proof
> 3. Reduce risk
> 4. Low-friction next step
> 5. Keep it short (150-250 words)

## PRO TIPS FROM RESEARCH

### Timing
- Apply within 1 hour of job posting (fresh jobs = higher response)
- Jobs over 24 hours old have 70% less chance

### Proof Works Better Than Claims
- Don't say "I specialize in React" — show BugSmasher
- Links beat descriptions every time

### Reduce Risk = Get Hired
- "I can show you a working demo first"
- "Happy to do a small paid trial"
- These work because they lower the client's fear

### Keep It Short
- 150-250 words is ideal
- Clients are scanning dozens of proposals
- Shorter = faster to read = higher chance of being read

### Personalize Every Time
- NEVER copy-paste without changing:
  - Name of their project/company
  - Specific requirement they mentioned
  - Their tech stack if mentioned
