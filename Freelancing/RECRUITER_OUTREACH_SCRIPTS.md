# 📬 RECRUITER OUTREACH SCRIPTS
## Getting Noticed Before You Apply

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS

**Fact:** 80% of jobs are filled through networks, not applications.
**Fact:** A referral is 10x more likely to get an interview than a cold application.
**Fact:** Recruiters DM candidates who look like they're in demand.

These scripts help you GET NOTICED so recruiters come to YOU.

---

## SCRIPT 1: LinkedIn Profile Optimization (Passive)

**Goal:** Make recruiters find YOU

**Actions:**
```
1. Set headline to: "Full-Stack Developer | React, TypeScript, Supabase | Available for Remote Work"
2. Turn ON #OpenToWork (visible to recruiters only, NOT public)
3. Set "Services" to "Offering services" with:
   - Freelance/Contract
   - Full-time remote
   - React, TypeScript, Node.js
4. Enable "Career Interests" — tell LinkedIn you're open
5. Keywords in About section: React, TypeScript, JavaScript, remote, available
6. Post 2x/week about what you're building
```

**Result:** Recruiters search these keywords. Your profile shows up. They DM you.

## LINKEDIN ACTIVITY PROTOCOL

**Do this daily to get recruiter attention:**

| Activity | Why It Works |
|----------|-------------|
| Post 1 dev-related update/day | Shows you're active + skilled |
| Comment on 5 recruiter/company posts | Gets on their radar |
| Engage with hiring managers' content | Algorithm shows you to them |
| Update profile photo + banner | Looks active and professional |
| Add new skills | Appears in skill-based searches |

**Warning:** Don't post about crypto trading or 6 ventures on LinkedIn job-search profile. Keep it professional and dev-focused.

## THE 80/20 RULE FOR OUTREACH

```
80% of applications get no response.
20% of your effort should be on the application.
80% should be on FOLLOWING UP + NETWORKING.

Application → 15 min
Follow-up DM  → 5 min (5 days later)
Engage with their content → 2 min/day
```
