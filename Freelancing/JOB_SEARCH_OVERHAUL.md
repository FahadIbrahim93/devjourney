```
100 applications → 15 responses → 5 interviews → 2 final rounds → 1 offer

100 × 15 min = 25 hours
25 hours ÷ 5 hours/week = 5 weeks to offer
```

### Application Quality Rules

1. **Never use "Easy Apply" without customizing** — Always add a note
2. **Apply within 24 hours of posting** — First applicants get 3x more views
3. **Reference the company's tech stack** — Show you read the JD
4. **Link to a RELEVANT project** — Not your personal brand site
5. **Follow up after 5 days** — "Just checking in..."
