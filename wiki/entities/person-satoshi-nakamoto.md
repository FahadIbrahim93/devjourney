# Satoshi Nakamoto

> Pseudonymous creator(s) of Bitcoin. Published the whitepaper in 2008, mined the genesis block in 2009, disappeared in 2011.

## Key Facts
- **Active:** 2008-2011
- **Identity:** Unknown (multiple candidates, no confirmed)
- **BTC holdings:** ~1M BTC (never moved)
- **Whitepaper:** "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System" (Oct 2008)

## Contributions
- Bitcoin protocol
- Proof-of-Work consensus
- Blockchain data structure
- Bitcoin Core (early)

## Why It Matters
- Solved [[concept-double-spend-problem]] without trusted third party
- [[concept-bitcoin-halving]] built into code
- Pseudonymous creator = trustless by design

## Open Questions
- Who is Satoshi? (Craig Wright claim rejected)
- Why did they disappear?
- Will the 1M BTC ever move?
