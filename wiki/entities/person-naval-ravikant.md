# Naval Ravikant

> AngelList co-founder, investor, philosopher. Famous for his tweetstorm on wealth and the Almanack of Naval Ravikant.

## Key Ideas
- **Wealth** = productized judgment + leverage + ownership
- **Leverage types:** capital, code, media, labor (3rd is permissionless)
- **Happiness** = peace + contentment + freedom from desire
- **Reading** = meta-skill

## Famous Quotes
- "Seek wealth, not money. Seek work that gives you leverage."
- "Code is leverage. Media is leverage. Use them."
- "Play long-term games with long-term people."
- "All the real benefits in life come from compound interest."

## Why We Cite
- His investment philosophy = how we should think about ventures
- His compounding framework = our shadow clone strategy
- "Build once, sell forever" = our product-first venture approach
