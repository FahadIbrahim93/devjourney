# Vitalik Buterin

> Co-founder of Ethereum. Proposer of Ethereum in 2013, launched 2015. Youngest crypto billionaire.

## Key Facts
- **Born:** 1994, Russia/Canada
- **Role:** Ethereum co-founder, researcher
- **Education:** Self-taught, dropped out of Waterloo
- **ETH holdings:** ~$1B+

## Contributions
- **Ethereum** — programmable blockchain, smart contracts
- **EIP process** — Ethereum Improvement Proposal system
- **zk-SNARKs, zk-STARKs** — privacy + scaling
- **Rollups** — L2 scaling solution

## Ideas
- "Decentralization is the core value"
- "Crypto is more than money — it's coordination"
- Rollup-centric roadmap (2020+)
