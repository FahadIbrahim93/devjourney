# Andrej Karpathy

> AI researcher, former Tesla AI Director, founder of Eureka Labs. Pioneer of the LLM Wiki pattern, micrograd, nanoGPT, and "Software 2.0."

## Key Facts
- **Born:** 1986, Slovakia/Russia
- **Role:** Founder of Eureka Labs (2024+)
- **Previous:** Director of AI at Tesla (2017-2022), OpenAI research scientist
- **Education:** PhD Stanford (deep learning), under Fei-Fei Li

## Famous Contributions
- **[[concept-llm-wiki]]** — markdown-based, agent-maintained knowledge base
- **micrograd** — tiny autograd engine, educational
- **nanoGPT** — minimal GPT training code
- **minbpe** — minimal BPE tokenizer
- **llm.c** — pure C implementation of GPT training
- **Software 2.0** — neural networks as new programming paradigm
