# DCA — Dollar Cost Averaging

> Invest a fixed amount at fixed intervals, regardless of price. The anti-FOMO strategy.

## How It Works
- **Set it:** $100/week, every Monday
- **Forget it:** Auto-buy via exchange
- **Stack:** 52 buys/year = ~$5,200/year

## Why It Works
- **Smooths volatility** — buy high and low over time
- **Removes emotion** — no "should I buy now?"
- **Lower stress** — works while you sleep
- **Beats timing** — 80%+ of timers lose to DCA

## When DCA Fails
- **Bear market entries** — still works long-term but slow
- **Single asset risk** — what if it goes to zero?
- **Fees** — high on small buys (use limit orders)

## Variations
- **Value Averaging** — buy more when low, less when high
- **Lump sum** — if you have cash now, statistically better
- **Halving-aware DCA** — increase buys 6 months pre-halving

## Our Strategy
- $50/day BTC, $25/day ETH
- Auto via Coinbase/CashApp
- Hold 4+ years
- Take 20% profits at 2x, 4x, 8x
