# Bitcoin Halving

> Every ~4 years (every 210,000 blocks), the Bitcoin mining reward is cut in half. Built into the protocol by [[person-satoshi-nakamoto]].

## How It Works
- **Genesis (2009):** 50 BTC per block
- **1st halving (2012):** 25 BTC
- **2nd halving (2016):** 12.5 BTC
- **3rd halving (2020):** 6.25 BTC
- **4th halving (2024):** 3.125 BTC
- **5th (2028 est):** 1.5625 BTC
- **Final (2140):** 0 BTC, 21M cap reached

## Why It Matters
- **Supply shock** — new BTC issuance drops, demand steady = price up
- **Miner economics** — reward halved, must rely on fees eventually
- **Cycles** — historically precedes bull runs (12-18 months lag)

## Historical Pattern
- Halving → accumulation → bull peak → bear → next halving
- 2012 halving → 2013 peak ($1,100)
- 2016 halving → 2017 peak ($20K)
- 2020 halving → 2021 peak ($69K)
- 2024 halving → 2025 peak (so far $108K)
