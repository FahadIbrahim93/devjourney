## 🚫 WHAT NOT TO SAY

❌ "I'm a developer looking for work"
❌ "Hire me"
❌ "I need a job"
❌ "Please check my portfolio"
❌ Long paragraphs
❌ Generic compliments ("great profile!")

## ✅ WHAT TO SAY

✅ "I saw your post about X..."
✅ "I built something similar..."
✅ "Here's what I learned..."
✅ "Worth a quick call?"
✅ Specific, concise, value-first
