## 2. SCOPE OF WORK
2.1 **Services:** Hope Theory shall provide development services as described in Exhibit A (Statement of Work) attached hereto.
2.2 **Deliverables:** All deliverables shall be listed in Exhibit A with specific acceptance criteria.
2.3 **Technology Stack:** Unless otherwise specified in Exhibit A, services will utilize:

   - Backend: Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL/MongoDB
   - Web3: ethers.js, web3.js, Solidity (basic)
   - AI/ML: OpenCode API, Gemini API, Ollama (local)
   - DevOps: Git, Docker, Vercel/AWS
2.4 **Zero-Slop Standard:** All code shall meet the following criteria:
   - **Documentation:** Complete inline comments, API docs, setup guide
   - **Testing:** Minimum 70% test coverage (unit + integration)
   - **Code Quality:** Zero linting errors (ESLint + Prettier)
   - **Build Success:** Production build succeeds without warnings
   - **Maintainability:** Class-based/modular architecture (not spaghetti)

## 3. TERM & TERMINATION
3.1 **Term:** This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until project completion unless terminated earlier.
3.2 **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate with 7 days written notice for material breach.
3.3 **Termination for Convenience:** Client may terminate with 14 days notice; Client pays for all work completed to date.
3.4 **Effect of Termination:** Hope Theory shall deliver all completed work product; Client pays for accepted deliverables.

## 4. COMPENSATION & PAYMENT TERMS
4.1 **Fees:** Client shall pay Hope Theory the fees set forth in Exhibit A.
4.2 **Payment Schedule:**
   - **New Clients:** 50% deposit upon signing, 50% upon delivery/acceptance
   - **Established Clients:** 30% deposit, 40% at midpoint, 30% upon delivery
   - **Retainer Clients:** Monthly in advance
4.3 **Currency:** All fees payable in United States Dollars (USD).
4.4 **Late Payment:** Undisputed invoices overdue >7 days accrue interest at 2% per week (26.08% APR).
4.5 **Payment Methods:** Wise, Payoneer, or direct crypto (USDT/USDC preferred).
4.6 **Expense Reimbursement:** Pre-approved expenses reimbursed with receipts.

5.3 **Third-Party Materials:** Hope Theory shall use commercially licensed or open-source components as needed; Client receives benefit of such licenses.
5.4 **Open Source Components:** Any open-source components used shall retain their original licenses.

6.2 **Obligations:** Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with at least reasonable care.
6.3 **Exclusions:** Information that is: (a) publicly known, (b) rightfully known without restriction, (c) independently developed.
6.4 **Term:** Obligations survive termination for 3 years.

## 7. WARRANTY & SUPPORT
7.1 **Workmanship Warranty:** Hope Theory warrants that Deliverables will materially conform to Exhibit A for 30 days after delivery.
7.2 **Bug Fixes:** During warranty period, Hope Theory will fix defects at no charge that:
   - Result from Hope Theory's workmanship
   - Are reported in writing with sufficient detail to reproduce
7.3 **Exclusions:** Warranty does not cover:
   - Changes to requirements after signing
   - Third-party software/hardware issues
   - Client-modified code
   - Usage outside documented specifications
7.4 **Ongoing Support:** Available via separate Maintenance Retainer Agreement.

## 8. SCOPE CREEP PROTECTION
8.1 **Change Orders:** Any change to Exhibit A requires written amendment signed by both parties.
8.2 **Minor Tweaks:** Changes under 30 minutes of work may be performed at Hope Theory's discretion without formal change order.
8.3 **Client Delay:** If Client delays feedback or approvals >48 hours, project timeline extends automatically.

## 9. COMMUNICATION & AVAILABILITY
9.1 **Response Time:** Hope Theory responds to Client inquiries within:
   - 4 hours during business hours (10AM-6PM UTC+6)
   - 12 hours outside business hours
9.2 **Overlap Hours:** Dedicated client availability: 8PM-12AM UTC+6 (10AM-2PM EST)
9.3 **Progress Updates:** Weekly status report delivered every Friday.
9.4 **Primary Communication:** Email, with optional Slack/Notion for real-time collaboration.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
10.1 **Cap:** Hope Theory's total liability shall not exceed the total fees paid under this Agreement.
10.2 **Exclusions:** Neither party liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, or punitive damages.
10.3 **Force Majeure:** Neither party liable for delays caused by circumstances beyond reasonable control.

## 11. GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTES
11.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Bangladesh.
11.2 **Dispute Resolution:** Parties shall first attempt good-faith negotiation; failing that, mediation in Dhaka.
11.3 **Venue:** Any litigation shall be brought in the courts of Dhaka, Bangladesh.

## 12. MISCELLANEOUS
