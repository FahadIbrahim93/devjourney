## 🧪 EXPERIMENT 1: Eid Bundle (Marjahans × SnapTrap)
**Venture Bridge:** [MARJAHANS] ↔ [SNAPTRAP]
**Type:** Product bundle
**Timeline:** Launch by July 1, 2026 (Eid season)

### Hypothesis
Customers who buy Marjahans jewelry (avg ৳1,800) + SnapTrap streetwear (avg ৳1,500) will buy a bundle at ৳2,800 (saving ৳500).

### Steps
1. Design 2-3 product pairings (e.g., 22K jhumkas + Trap Hoodie, pearl earrings + Rise Tee)
2. Create limited edition Eid collection (5-7 pieces total)
3. Bundle pricing: ৳2,800 for jewelry + tee combo
4. Marketing: Cross-promote on both IG accounts, both X accounts
5. Email list cross-promotion
6. Joint influencer campaign (5 micro-influencers)

### Success Metrics
- 50 bundle sales = ৳140,000 revenue
- 30% new customers acquired for one or both brands
- IG followers +300 (combined)
- UGC: 10+ tagged posts

### Risk
- If one product doesn't sell, the bundle doesn't sell
- Mitigation: Start with 2 bundles, not 5

## 🧪 EXPERIMENT 2: Web3 Financing Pilot (Build × Crypto)
**Venture Bridge:** [BUILD] → [CRYPTO]
**Type:** Service innovation
**Timeline:** Test with 1 client by August 2026

### Hypothesis
[BUILD] clients (real estate developers) will pay for the option to use crypto (BTC/ETH) for milestone payments, especially international clients.

### Steps
1. Update [BUILD] proposal template with "Crypto payment option" line
2. Offer 1-2% discount for crypto payment (incentivize)
3. Set up crypto wallet for [BUILD] payments
4. Pilot with 1 international client (or local client comfortable with crypto)
5. Document the process, fees, time
6. If successful, expand to all [BUILD] proposals

### Success Metrics
- 1 client accepts crypto payment
- First crypto invoice paid (any amount)
- Process documented in SOP
- Cost savings vs traditional banking: 2%+ (international wires)

### Risk
- Crypto volatility between invoice and payment
- Client unfamiliarity with crypto
- Tax/legal complexity

### Mitigation
- Use stablecoins (USDC) for invoicing
- Educate client with 1-page guide
- Work with accountant for tax treatment

**Type:** NFT-linked physical drop
**Timeline:** Q1 2027 (after core drops are stable)

NFT-linked physical streetwear drops will command 30%+ premium and attract crypto-native buyers.
