## 🎯 THE BRAND

SnapTrap is a streetwear label born out of Hope Theory. Limited drops, culture-first, community-driven. We don't chase trends — we interpret them.

### Core Value Proposition
- Limited drops (50-300 units per drop)
- Heavyweight cotton, real embroidery
- Made in Dhaka, designed for the streets
- Drop-based scarcity (every drop is finite)
- Community co-creation (fans vote on next design)

### Business Model
- **Drops:** 3-4 per year, themed (Q2, Q3, Q4, Holiday)
- **Pricing:** ৳1,200-৳2,800 BDT per piece
- **Fulfillment:** Print-on-demand or small-batch
- **Margin:** 55-60% per piece
- **Channels:** Shopify (planned), Instagram, TikTok
