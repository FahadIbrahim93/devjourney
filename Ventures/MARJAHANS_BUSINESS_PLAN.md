Marjahans is an online jewelry shop selling curated pieces with storytelling and aesthetic focus. Active business with real sales, real customers, real product catalog.

- DM-to-order Shopify store, Instagram-first

- **Inventory:** Curated, story-driven pieces
- **Pricing:** Mid-range (৳1,500-৳15,000 BDT per piece)
- **Fulfillment:** Dhaka showroom + national delivery
